Tretā-yuga placement through Rāma is a traditional cross-text linkage; it has not been converted into a BCE date.

Undated witnesses and dating assumptions
  • Taittirīya Saṃhitā 7.5.5: later Vedic reception; our dataset has no sourced numeric date for this layer.
  • Other Tamil and Purāṇic witnesses remain unplotted until their specific dates are sourced.
  • Bars show proposed composition or witness intervals, not manuscript ages or dates of narrated events.
  • The historical-person band is conditional: existence alone does not establish Vedic authorship or a lifespan.
  • Century labels use approximate numeric bounds. Astronomical year numbering is used internally; the display has no year zero.
