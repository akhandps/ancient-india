# Jāmbavant identity and enrichment experiment

Ranks use exact betweenness on a fixed union node universe. All other identity choices are held fixed.

| Scenario | VP/aligned record rank, all edges | Without attendance: rank / degree | Without attendance and broad kinship: rank / degree |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| original | 14 | 3 / 6 | 5 / 3 |
| existing_jambavant_split | 890 | 865 / 3 | None / 1 |
| enriched_source_local | 973 | 922 / 3 | None / 1 |
| enriched_align_jambavant | 9 | 2 / 16 | 4 / 10 |
| enriched_align_named_figures | 9 | 2 / 13 | 4 / 8 |

The first two enriched scenarios keep Kṛṣṇa and other new Bhāgavata figures source-local. The last scenario additionally aligns the explicitly listed named figures. Changes are conditional on those mappings and targeted corpus enrichment, not independent confirmation.

See `analysis.json` for all four focal dossiers, neighbor claim IDs, scores, and input hashes. No merged graph is written over the original dataset.
